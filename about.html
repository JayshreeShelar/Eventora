Our Story

Born from a love of meaningful gatherings

Eventora began with a simple belief: the best events feel personal. A family dinner, a wedding procession and a company launch all matter most when guests feel genuinely welcome.

Since then our studio has grown into a team of designers, planners and coordinators who share one habit: listening first, then building the day around what we heard.

Our Vision

Events that people remember for how they felt

We want every host to enjoy their own event, and every guest to leave feeling looked after. Beauty matters, but ease and warmth matter more.

Planning Philosophy

Design the feeling, then plan the details

We start with the atmosphere you want, then work backwards through layout, florals, timing and vendors until every detail supports it.
